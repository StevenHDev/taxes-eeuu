## Why we ask for this

Federal law requires us to obtain your consent before we use or disclose your tax return information for purposes other than preparing your tax return. If you do not consent, we cannot use your information for those purposes.

## What information

What you tell us in the interview (on WhatsApp or in this portal) and the documents you upload: personal and family details, income, expenses, and the IRS forms you received.

## How we use it

- To prepare and review your tax return for the year.
- To use artificial-intelligence assistance tools that help us organize your answers and read your documents. These tools are provided by an outside vendor that processes the information on our behalf.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

## How long it lasts

This consent is valid for the tax year of this return and for one year after you sign it, unless you withdraw it earlier.

## Your rights

- You are not required to sign. If you do not, we can still prepare your return with a preparer, without the assistance tools.
- You can withdraw your consent at any time by telling us in writing.

If you believe your tax return information has been used or disclosed without your permission, you may contact the Treasury Inspector General for Tax Administration (TIGTA) at 1-800-366-4484 or complaints@tigta.treas.gov.
